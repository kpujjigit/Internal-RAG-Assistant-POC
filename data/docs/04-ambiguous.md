# Travel & Expense (Mock) — Known Ambiguities

- Meals are reimbursable up to **$60/day** while traveling.
- Meals are reimbursable up to **$75/day** in high-cost cities.

Notes:
- “High-cost city” is not consistently defined across teams.
- Some teams interpret “high-cost” as SF/NYC/Seattle; others use a finance-published list.
- This doc is intentionally inconsistent to test “low confidence / needs clarification” behavior.
